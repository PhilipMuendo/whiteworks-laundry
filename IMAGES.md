# Image credits & replacement guide

All photography in `/assets/images` is **placeholder stock**, graded to one consistent monochrome look
(desaturated ~86%, filmic S-curve, cool shadows / neutral highlights, blacks lifted to `#080808`).

**Replace with the client's own plant photography before launch.** Real photos of the actual facility,
vehicles and team will outperform any stock image. Keep the filename to swap in place, or import a new
file in `config/site.ts`. To match the grade: `python scripts/grade.py <raw-photos> assets/images`.
